> **Disclaimer**
> This repository is for personal study and reference only. The official source of truth is always the live EKL training material on the TU Delft SharePoint. Always follow the most recent on-site safety guidance and instructions from the EKL Safety Officer / Emergency Response Team. Rules and procedures may change.

## How `ANSWERS.md` is structured

Each of the 24 questions has:

1. **The selected option(s)** — concise multiple-choice answers.
2. **A short explanation** — why those options are correct (and why the rest are not).
3. **A direct PDF citation** — exact wording quoted from the relevant slide deck, so each answer is traceable to a primary source.

A summary table at the bottom maps each question to its source PDF for quick lookup.
